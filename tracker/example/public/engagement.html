Scroll & engagement

Scroll this tall page to raise max scroll depth (rounded to 5%). On pagehide / visibility hidden (when pagehide is unavailable), the tracker sends ty=engagement with d (active seconds) and sc (scroll %). Idle time (>2 min without scroll) and time spent in a hidden tab are excluded from duration.

Try: scroll halfway → switch tabs for a few seconds → return → leave the page (or navigate away). Inspect the engagement POST / beacon.
